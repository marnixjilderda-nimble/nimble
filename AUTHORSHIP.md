# Authorship and ownership of Nimble

**Owner and sole author:** Marnix Pieter Ayolt Lodewijk Jilderda, Madrid, Spain.

## What Nimble is

Nimble is a recruitment and business-development "steering wheel": a single-page web
application that gives recruiters a fast overview of, and control over, all their ongoing
processes. Earlier working names were PipeTree, Pace and HQ.

## How it was created

- Nimble was conceived, designed and built by Marnix Jilderda **on his own initiative**.
  No employer asked for it, commissioned it or instructed him to build it.
- Software development is **not part of his employment**. He is employed as Head of Client
  Recruitment EMEA; his contract does not cover developing software.
- It was built with **his own accounts and tools**: his personal Anthropic (Claude) account,
  his own GitHub account (`marnixjilderda-nimble`) and his own hosting (GitHub Pages).
  No employer budget or developer was used to create it.
- Before Nimble there was a very basic personal tracker (v2) that he made with Microsoft
  Copilot, to keep track of his own work. Nimble is a separate, completely different program,
  built with a Claude subscription he pays for himself. The two share only the general idea
  of dragging candidates through a pipeline. Nimble can import data exported from the old
  tracker.
- Work on Nimble is recorded since at least early October 2026 (personal claude.ai project
  "Nimble", then this repository from 18 November 2026). Every change is in this
  repository's history with its date and time.
- The design, the workflows and every product decision are his. AI tools (Claude) wrote
  code under his direction, as a tool, on his personal account.
